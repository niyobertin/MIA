## 2. What we collect

| Data | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Name, email, phone, role, PIN (stored only as a one-way hash) | To sign you in and control what each team member can do |
| Business profile | Shop name, business code, currency, time zone | To set up your shop |
| Business records you enter | Products, prices, stock, sales, purchases, expenses, payments, daily closings | To provide the app's core features and reports |
| Customer and supplier details you enter | Name, phone, email, address, amounts owed | To track credit and debts for your shop |
| Activity log | Which team member made which change, and when | So owners can audit their shop |
| Technical | IP address and request times in server logs | Security and preventing abuse |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos or camera, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers **(confirm before each release)**.

## 3. Where data is stored

- **On the phone:** records are saved on the device first, so the app works offline.
- **On our server:** records are copied to our server when the phone is online, so they are backed up and shared with your team. The server is hosted by [HOSTING PROVIDER] in [COUNTRY] **(confirm)**. If the server is outside Rwanda, we keep the authorisation required for cross-border storage **(confirm)**.

## 4. How long we keep it

We keep business records for as long as your account is active, so your history and reports stay complete. Deleted accounts are removed within [30] days, and their backups within [90] days. We keep records longer only where the law requires it, for example tax rules.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Your team:** people you add to your business can see its data, depending on their role.
- **Service providers:** our hosting provider, only to run the service.
- **Authorities:** only when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

## 6. Security

- PINs are stored only as one-way hashes. Accounts lock after repeated wrong PINs.
- Connections to our server are encrypted with HTTPS.
- Each business's data is kept separate from other businesses.
- We back up the server database every day.

If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify NCSA and the people affected within the time the law requires **(confirm: 48 hours to NCSA)**.
